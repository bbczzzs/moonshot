Moonshot

A live crash game. Your Rare Friend pilots the rocket, a crew of real Generations Friends hitches a ride, and 10% of every stake burns as fuel, win or lose. A new launch every ~15 seconds.

Play now ▶ Watch the video Full rules

Playing needs a browser wallet on Robinhood mainnet (4663) holding a hardwired Rare Friends Generations NFT (gen ≥ 1), the FriendSDK ownership gate. No transactions: all RF in the game is simulated demo RF.

How it plays

1

Board

A 6-second window: stake RF and your Friend climbs into the cockpit while the crew walks the gantry.

2

Liftoff

10% of every stake burns as fuel. The multiplier climbs past the Moon (2×), Mars (5×) and Saturn (10×).

3

Eject

Cash out any time and parachute down with ride × multiplier, or eject half and let the rest fly.

4

Or crash

Everyone still aboard burns with the rocket. Crashes feed the Launch Pool that pays the ejectors.
